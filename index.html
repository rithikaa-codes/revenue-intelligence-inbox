Deliberately Rejected Product Decision

Rejected Alternative: Enforcing a Strict "One Route Per Conversation" Constraint

Why it was considered: Traditional CRM pipelines force every activity into a single category (e.g. either "Sales Lead" OR "Customer Support") to maintain clean 1:1 reporting.

Why it was rejected: Real revenue conversations routinely serve multiple organizational purposes. For example:
• C07 is both a channel partnership negotiation (Partner Development) and an active task (Customer Follow-up).
• M07 is both customer issue support (Customer Follow-up) and an urgent engineering bug report (Internal / Vendor Note).
• M03 is both an active enterprise sales deal (Deal Next Steps) and a coaching boundary on executive gatekeepers (Sales Coaching).
Forcing a single category would destroy cross-departmental visibility and lose vital follow-up context.

Trade-off Accepted: Total assigned routing counts (e.g. 52 across 44 records) exceed the number of unique source records. To prevent confusion, the Overview dashboard and Methodology panel explicitly display the distinct denominators and explain this design choice transparently.

Calculated as distinct count of (source_type, source_id) across all applied deliveries. Re-importing batches or altering record sequence never increments this total. 30 calls + 14 meetings = 44 unique records.

2. Usable Supplied Text Coverage (Denominator = 44)

Represents records with non-empty conversation text. In initial delivery, 17 records have text (10 calls, 7 meetings; 38.6%). After delivery update-002, M07 supplies 27 turns, raising coverage to 18 (40.9%). Missing text does not mean a call never took place; it reflects extract availability.

3. Missing Usable Text (Intake Queue)

27 initially (26 post-update). These records contain metadata only. Per DATA_CONTRACT.md, substantive analysis is withheld rather than fabricated.

4. Routing Counts vs Unique Records

Because SpendNest supports multi-destination routing (e.g. a call supporting both Deal Next Steps and Customer Follow-up), the sum of route assignments exceeds 44. Each route card shows the exact number of records assigned to that specific destination.
